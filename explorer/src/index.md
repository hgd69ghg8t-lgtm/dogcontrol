# NZ Dog Control Explorer

<p class="lede">Interactive views of New Zealand's annual dog control statistics, published by MCERT. They go deeper than the <a href="../index.html">dashboard</a>.</p>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2">
  <a class="card" href="./map.html" style="text-decoration:none">
    <h2>Council map</h2>
    <p>Any of the 16 measures mapped across all 67 councils, as counts or rates per 1,000 dogs. Step through the years from 2013, and click a council for its trend and most common breeds.</p>
  </a>
  <a class="card" href="./breeds.html" style="text-decoration:none">
    <h2>Breed explorer</h2>
    <p>A breed × council heatmap: each breed's share of a council's dogs, menacing and dangerous rates, and de-sexing and micro-chipping. Includes a profile and map for any breed, plus CSV downloads.</p>
  </a>
</div>

<p class="muted">Years are financial years ending 30 June. The 2024 National Dog Database snapshot is much lower than the years either side for almost every council; treat 2024 with caution.</p>
